sequence between the doorway and railing; the ordinary player remains indoors.

clean edges. Very low alpha (1–2/255) RGB fringe is retained in the original;
actual GPU blending, rather than a black transparent-image preview, determines
its visibility. No full-body scaling animation or raster post-processing.
