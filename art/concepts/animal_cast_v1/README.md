## Review notes

The approved hero/garden painting guided the warm ivory paper, dry-brush gouache marks, natural golden lighting, and storybook interpretation. Original animal assets guided species, wool, coat colors, and personalities. The sleepy sheep is deliberately broader, lower, and more heavy-lidded than the affectionate sheep.

The goose is alert but still quite gentle; review whether it needs a more nosy expression. Its design must remain clearly a white goose, distinct from the yellow duckling.

This is an opaque paper approval sheet, not transparent cutouts. Baselines and scenery marks belong only to this presentation. Before runtime production, each approved design needs an individual transparent source and explicit ground anchor. The llama's idle, happy, annoyed, and smirk faces must use one identical canonical body, canvas, scale, and feet placement. No animation work is part of this deliverable.
