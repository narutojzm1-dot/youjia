- Identity/style reference: the project's already approved, right-facing white goose at `assets/holiday/characters/cast_v2/goose.png`. The original remains the active/alert cel. External sitting-geese photographs were examined only for anatomy; no photographic pixels are included here.
- Method: two separate high-resolution transparent painted posture variations of **that same** goose. One is quiet upright with both wings folded and a closed beak; the other lies with its belly in contact with the ground, its feet tucked and wings folded. ## Riding flap pair — REQ-20261002-015 (issue #64)

- Date: 2026-10-03; product confirmed the mount-flap moment needs **two alternating cels**, not one, because the encounter alternates frames on a 0.28s cadence.
- Method honesty: both are freshly painted, separate full-body transparent cels of the same approved goose. Neither is a scaled/deformed copy of `goose.png`, `goose_calm.png` or `goose_rest.png`.
- Matte pipeline (fully disclosed): the source renders carried a baked light-grey checkerboard. It was removed in three passes — (1) 8px-tile dematte of the background, (2) de-contamination of translucent edge pixels by inverting the composite against the local checker colour, (3) a bounded flood from the transparent region that eats only *neutral* grey/white pixels, which removes the partially-covered checker tiles that survive as white confetti glued to the outline while sparing interior white highlights (they are warm-tinted and unreachable by the flood). Evidence: after cleaning, zero transparent pixels carry a checker colour, the translucent ring shows no 8px periodicity (parity brightness delta ≤0.5/255), and among the remaining neutral translucent pixels near-white (252,252,252) — the goose's own feather anti-aliasing — dominates at 17-20% while the checker-tile grey (204,204,204) accounts for only 1-2%, so the residue reads as plumage, not board.
- Scale honesty: the masters are 1024×1024 and the runtime cels are 1254×1254, so this is a modest **upsample** (×1.22, LANCZOS), not a downsample like the 1920→1254 path used by the approved posture cels. Edge softness from that upsample is a known, accepted trade-off; a higher-resolution repaint is listed as follow-up work if the cel reads soft in engine captures.
- Anchors: the down-stroke cel is integer-translated so its foot-contact anchor lands on the up-stroke anchor; the residual between the two measured anchors is 0.48px (sub-pixel, invisible on frame swap). The down-stroke anchor x is deliberately copied from the up-stroke rather than recomputed per-cel: flap alternation must not shift the contact point against the horse back.
